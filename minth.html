About the project

MINTH Adventure Guide helps people find their way around a museum, learn through activities and get help during their visit. We built it as team 404 Culture for IdeateCOMM.

What I did

  • Contributed UI/UX design across the guide
  • Developed the visitor chatbot with n8n workflows and AI assistance tools
  • Developed the Contact page, accessibility settings and footer

Accessibility settings

  • Larger text
  • Dark mode
  • High contrast
  • Reduced motion
